Frequently Asked Questions

What is your interior design process?

Our process begins with a detailed consultation to understand your vision. We then proceed to space planning, 2D/3D visualizations, material selection, and finally, precise execution and handover. For turnkey projects, we manage everything end-to-end.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes, the initial consultation and site visit are complimentary. We discuss your requirements, budget, and timeline to propose a suitable design strategy.

Which areas in Mumbai do you serve?

We serve all major areas across Mumbai including Andheri, Bandra, Goregaon, Powai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, South Mumbai, and surrounding regions.

Do you undertake renovation projects?

Absolutely. We specialize in comprehensive home and office renovations, including civil changes, plumbing, electrical work, and custom furniture manufacturing.
